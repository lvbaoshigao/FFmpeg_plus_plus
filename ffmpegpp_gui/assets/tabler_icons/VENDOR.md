# Tabler Icons

The SVGs in [outline/](outline/) come from [Tabler Icons at revision 85e870d](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/tree/85e870dbe8680c2d6deefd062122d30ef1544a41/icons/outline). They are bundled under the [MIT License](LICENSE) and retain the upstream filenames and content.

The [TablerIcon widget](../../lib/widgets/tabler_icon.dart) applies Flutter theme colors to this subset for primary navigation and pipeline node types. To add an icon, copy the SVG from the pinned upstream revision into [outline/](outline/) and register its filename in `TablerGlyph`.

The original Tabler logic-gate artwork is stored separately in [rele/logic_gates/](../../rele/logic_gates/).
